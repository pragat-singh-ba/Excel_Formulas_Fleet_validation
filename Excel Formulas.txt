Excel Formulas

1. For creating the Group Key:

For Finder:

=TRIM(CLEAN(TEXT(Q2, "yyyy-mm-dd ") & TEXT(O2, "hh:mm") & "_" & M2 & "_" & J2))
(Where Q2=Converted date, O2= transaction time, M2=Authorisation Code, H2=Terminal number)

For settled: 

=TRIM(CLEAN(TEXT(Q2, "yyyy-mm-dd ") & TEXT(M2, "hh:mm") & "_" & K2 & "_" & H2))
(Where Q2=Converted date, M2=original transaction time, K2=Approval Code, H2=Terminal ID)

2. For Matching:

For Finder:

=IF(COUNTIF(settled!$R$2:$R$66926,R2)>0,1,0) (adjust the Column count accordingly)

For settled:

=IF(COUNTIF(finder!$R$2:$R$65938,R23107)>0,1,0) (adjust the Column count accordingly)
